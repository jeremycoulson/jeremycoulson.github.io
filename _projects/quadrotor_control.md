Quadrotor helicopters are an ideal testbed for data-driven control methods: they are highly nonlinear, subject to aerodynamic disturbances, and require fast real-time control. Our group has demonstrated that DeePC can successfully control quadrotors using only input-output data, without requiring a first-principles model of the vehicle dynamics.

We have shown that DeePC can track complex trajectories such as figure-eight patterns, handling the significant nonlinearities and disturbances encountered in real flight. We have also developed robust adaptive model predictive control schemes for quadrotors that combine data-driven methods with online adaptation. Beyond research, we have used quadrotor control as an educational platform, developing hands-on laboratory courses at ETH Zurich where students learn control theory through real drone experiments.

Key papers include data-enabled predictive control for quadcopters (IJRNC 2021), robust adaptive MPC for quadrotors (ECC 2021), and hands-on quadcopter education (IFAC 2020).
