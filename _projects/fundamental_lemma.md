Willems' fundamental lemma is the cornerstone of behavioral data-driven control. It states that a single, sufficiently rich input-output trajectory of a linear time-invariant system can parameterize all possible trajectories of that system. The richness condition, known as persistency of excitation, determines whether the collected data is informative enough for control design.

Our research has developed a quantitative notion of persistency of excitation that goes beyond the binary "excited or not" characterization. We provide a quantitative and constructive proof of the fundamental lemma that reveals how the degree of excitation in the data directly impacts the robustness of the resulting data-driven representation. This analysis provides practical guidance on how much and what kind of data to collect for reliable data-driven control.

Key papers include a quantitative notion of persistency of excitation (IEEE CSL 2022), a quantitative proof of Willems' fundamental lemma (ACC 2023), and the robust fundamental lemma (MTNS 2022).
