The control community has traditionally followed two paradigms: indirect data-driven control, where a model is first identified from data and then used for controller design, and direct data-driven control, where controllers are designed directly from data without an explicit identification step. Our research reveals deep connections between these two approaches.

We have shown that specific regularization choices in the DeePC optimization problem recover well-known indirect control methods, including certainty-equivalent control with identified models. This unifying perspective bridges decades of work in system identification and adaptive control with modern behavioral data-driven methods, providing both theoretical insight and practical guidance on when to use direct versus indirect approaches.

Key paper: Bridging direct and indirect data-driven control formulations via regularizations and relaxations (IEEE TAC 2022).
